## Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Guidelines
- Keep under 50 characters (mobile optimization)
- Use emojis sparingly (1 per subject max)
- A/B test everything
- Personalize when possible: "[Name], ..."
- Create urgency (when appropriate): "48 hours left"
- Ask questions: "Still struggling with budgeting?"

### Email Design Specs
- Max width: 600px
- Font: PT Sans (match brand)
- Colors: Match app palette (#74b49b primary)
- Mobile-first design (60% open on mobile)
- Single clear CTA per email
- Alt text for all images

### Sending Cadence
- Welcome sequence: Days 0, 2, 5, 7, 10
- Newsletter: 1x per month (first Sunday)
- Product updates: As needed (max 1x per week)
- Re-engagement: After 14 days inactive

### Segmentation Strategy
- New users (0-30 days)
- Active users (logged in last 7 days)
- Power users (top 20% by activity)
