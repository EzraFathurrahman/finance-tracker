## Design Specifications

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** `#74b49b` (Soft teal)
- **Background:** `#e0f4f1` (Light teal)
- **Accent:** `#5d9a7e` (Muted green)
- **Text:** `#1f2937` (Dark gray)
- **Header Gradient:** Blue gradient (left to right, as per app)

### Typography
- **Headings:** PT Sans Bold
- **Body:** PT Sans Regular
- **Monospace (for code/numbers):** System monospace

### Spacing
- Section padding: `96px` vertical, `24px` horizontal
- Card spacing: `24px` gap
- Mobile: Reduce to `48px` vertical

### Components to Reuse from App
- Button styles (from shadcn/ui)
- Card components
- Theme toggle in header
- Language toggle in header

### Animations
- Scroll-triggered fade-ins
- Hover effects on cards
- Smooth transitions on theme/language switch
- AI processing animation (loading states)

### Responsive Breakpoints
- Mobile: `< 768px`
- Tablet: `768px - 1024px`
- Desktop: `> 1024px`
